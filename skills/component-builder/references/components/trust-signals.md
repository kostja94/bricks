# Trust Signals

## Responsibility

Trust signals reduce a specific, legitimate uncertainty with verifiable evidence such as certifications, customer logos, security status, guarantees, ratings, or operational facts.

## Decisions

Identify the uncertainty first, then choose evidence the target audience can verify. A generic row of badges is not a substitute for product proof or transparent policy.

## Constraints

- Use only current, authorized marks and accurate claims.
- Distinguish certification, membership, compatibility, review score, and customer relationship.
- Link to verification where it materially helps.
- Do not imply endorsement from ordinary tool usage or unsupported logo placement.

## Verify

Check authorization, expiry, source links, claim wording, contrast, accessible names, mobile density, and whether the evidence addresses the nearby decision.
