# FAQ Section

## Responsibility

An FAQ section organizes real questions and direct answers within another page. It resolves recurring uncertainty at the point where it matters; it is not a container for unrelated keywords or a substitute for missing primary content.

## Content Contract

Each item needs a stable identifier, a user-centered question, and an answer whose first sentence gives the conclusion. Category and anchor fields are useful only when the page genuinely needs grouping or direct links. Keep visible content as the source for any derived structured data.

## Question Selection and Answer Design

Choose questions by the page's topic and the user's intent, not by a requirement to mention the product. Editorial and educational pages may answer subject-matter questions without a product connection. Product and task pages should resolve relevant decisions, requirements, limitations, and troubleshooting. Pricing pages should address real buying questions such as billing units, included usage, overages, renewal, cancellation, and refunds only where verified terms support the answer. Do not invent policies to fill an FAQ.

Use first-party search queries, support questions, user research, and relevant live search results as evidence. People Also Ask can reveal related question phrasing and follow-up intent; inspect the target query, language, market, and research date when using it. Treat observed questions as candidates, filter for relevance and redundancy, and do not label inferred questions as observed PAA results. Do not copy competitors' answers or collect unrelated questions solely for traffic.

Write the question in natural user language. Start the answer with a direct, self-contained conclusion, naming the subject clearly enough to remain understandable when quoted. Add the conditions, evidence, example, or next step needed to make it accurate. Use a short paragraph for a definition, a list for steps, or a table for a genuine comparison when the project's content renderer supports it. Do not impose a universal answer length or six-question quota. If answering requires a substantial explanation, give it an appropriate main-content section and keep only a useful concise clarification in the FAQ; do not duplicate that section.

## Search and AI Visibility

Design answers to be useful, readable, and accurately extractable for search and AI systems. Clear questions, direct answers, explicit entities and units, supported claims, and sources or dates for changeable facts help readers assess a passage independently. These are content-quality practices, not a guarantee of citation, ranking, or special search appearance.

Distinguish People Also Ask, featured snippets, FAQ rich results, and AI answers. They are different surfaces; an FAQ component or FAQPage markup does not automatically qualify content for all of them. Google selects featured snippets algorithmically; there is no markup that designates a passage as a featured snippet. Google's AI search guidance requires no special AI schema or additional optimization beyond applicable search fundamentals. Other platforms may have different documented requirements; check the target platform rather than generalizing Google's rules.

Before making visibility claims, verify the target search feature's current official requirements. Check that intended public pages are crawlable and indexable, important answers are available as text, and any structured output matches visible content. Measure observed visibility separately from content implementation; preview pages intentionally excluded from indexing are not an SEO performance test.

## Presentation

- Use expanded answers when the set is short or immediate visibility matters.
- Use accessible disclosure or accordion behavior when space and scanning justify it.
- Use groups or an anchor index for distinct topics; consider a dedicated FAQ page when the section becomes a knowledge base.

Presentation is a variant choice. Accordion is not part of the FAQ Section identity.

## Constraints

- Do not publish empty, duplicate, hidden, or keyword-manufactured questions.
- Important product, pricing, legal, or safety information must not exist only inside a collapsed FAQ.
- If multiple FAQ sections exist on one page, page-level structured data ownership must prevent contradictory or duplicate output.
- Google Search no longer displays FAQ rich results; do not add FAQ structured data to promise that appearance. Other consumers may still justify machine-readable FAQ data when the project has a concrete requirement.

## Verify

Check question relevance to the page's intent, evidence behind claimed PAA findings, answer-first writing, verified policies, and overlap with main content or other FAQ items. Then check keyboard disclosure behavior, heading order, direct links where supported, server-rendered answer availability, long answers, missing items, multiple sections, and visible-data agreement for any structured output.
